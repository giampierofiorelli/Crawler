PEE — R3 authorisation and consolidated scientific knowledge translation requirements

R2 is accepted as completed experimental infrastructure, with its documented limitations. Proceed with R3 under the existing roadmap, preserving all prior owner decisions, scientific research, working capabilities and experimental history.

Materials: No new attachments or external acquisition are authorised. First inventory the owner’s scientific teaching documents actually available to you, recording their exact filenames, paths, versions and accessibility. Prefer the proposed air-purifier, coffee-machine and air-fryer packages if accessible; otherwise select representative existing documents. Do not recreate missing owner documents or claim to have examined unavailable material.

Core R3 objective

PEE must progressively learn to translate scientific documents into faithful, reusable, operational knowledge. This is not simply extracting terminology, adding relations manually or asking Claude to interpret every future category.

Use the established engineering research throughout R3, including these methodological principles:

* CommonKADS: distinguish domain knowledge, inference mechanisms and the tasks that apply that knowledge.
* Rosetta Statements: preserve the meaning of scientific assertions through explicit, structured representations that can be reconstructed and checked against the original statement.
* ORKG: represent research questions, methods, experimental conditions, findings and their comparability.
* NeOn: reuse and extend existing knowledge structures instead of creating duplicate ontologies, isolated category vocabularies or parallel engines.
* Continue applying Functional Basis, SAREF, QK, KR, provenance, counterexamples and independent evaluation where appropriate.

These are engineering references, not mandatory libraries, services or architectural migrations.

Required work

1. Establish a source-first inventory of the important knowledge contained in representative owner documents: scientific mechanisms, physical and chemical relationships, causal and conditional claims, experimental methods, compound procedures, quantitative evidence, qualitative expert judgements, limitations, conflicts, applicability and consumer outcomes.
2. Compare that inventory with the existing store and executable knowledge. Distinguish correctly translated, partly translated, distorted, missing, and represented-but-unused knowledge. Do not infer completeness from the number of stored relations.
3. Determine what the current declarative structures can represent faithfully, and identify genuine representational gaps. Preserve distinctions between general scientific principles, source-specific observations, expert judgements, hypotheses and verified evidence. Avoid promoting owner teaching material to independently validated scientific proof.
4. Develop the smallest general mechanism inside PEE software for acquiring and representing this knowledge from documents with provenance, scope, uncertainty and reversible validation. Prefer reuse of QK/KR/WD/QE/FC and the existing store. Claude manually preparing a knowledge inventory is not evidence that PEE itself can perform knowledge acquisition.
5. Test semantic fidelity in both directions: original document to structured knowledge, and structured knowledge back to a checkable scientific statement. Measure omissions, distortions and unjustified inference, not just concept extraction.
6. Demonstrate operational benefit through controlled before/after and knowledge-ablation experiments: reading, evidence linkage, quantitative and qualitative interpretation, product attribution, condition recognition, justified conclusions and abstentions. Account for limitations inherited from the base reader rather than crediting or blaming the knowledge mechanism for them.
7. Use genuinely held-out sections or groups wherever possible. Do not call same-model blind annotations independent human scientific validation. Preserve first-run results, report spent data honestly and separate synthetic, development and independent evidence.

Use the previously identified problems (empty-run versus cooking-task energy, SDR/HDR, aged-filter conditions and compound cost procedures) as diagnostic cases, not as prompts to create category-specific recognisers. Measure whether general mechanisms solve them and transfer to other contexts.

Safeguards and completion criteria

Remain shadow-only. Do not unlock ranking, migrate wholesale, discard legacy capabilities, acquire external pages, introduce paid dependencies or create a separate knowledge architecture.

Preserve source references already present in the documents, but do not retrieve their original scientific sources at this stage. Respect existing rights and retention policies.

Report separately what Claude manually analysed, what PEE implemented, what changed operationally, and what independent evidence demonstrates. Report the original knowledge coverage and the residual omissions, and quantify improvements and regressions.

R3 is successful only if a reusable scientific translation/consultation mechanism produces a measured improvement beyond adding vocabulary, while maintaining traceable, bounded conclusions. If independent evidence or required documents are unavailable, report the limitation rather than inventing validation.

Choose the implementation and experiments autonomously within these requirements. Preserve this consolidated R3 brief in the permanent skill and roadmap, without superseding unrelated earlier decisions.
