## Claude's reading of it (not part of the decision)

- **What was wrong.** S86c and S87a found every correction on the same spent rows, mostly from one publisher (S80,
  HouseFresh), and measured each one there. That is the failure the skill's generalisation method forbids:
  - Part B §2: a principle stated over words or names, not over relations or types;
  - Part B §3: no group split, no worst group;
  - Part D §4: validation on the cases that suggested the principle.
- **What S87b must show.** Group-wise generalisation: each mechanism is developed on one group only, frozen, and then
  measured on the categories and publishers it never saw. The report covers the worst group and a category that was
  never taught. Coverage and precision are reported together.
