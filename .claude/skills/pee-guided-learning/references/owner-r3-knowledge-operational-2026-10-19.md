PEE — Continue R3: Make Scientific Knowledge Operational

Continue the established R1–R7 roadmap. R3 remains the current phase. Do not introduce a parallel architecture or prematurely implement R5.

The S80 results show that reading and error reporting improved, but scientific knowledge has not yet demonstrated a generalisable improvement in decisions.

Your next objective is to determine why, and correct the underlying general mechanism.

1. Trace the existing knowledge-to-inference path across QK, KR, category teaching, translated statements, reading, request understanding and advice. Identify where acquired scientific knowledge is unavailable, ignored, incorrectly interpreted or unable to affect a conclusion.
2. First test reasoning on correctly structured scientific facts, independently of natural-language recognition. Determine whether the existing inference mechanisms can apply general principles involving types, conditions, comparability, negation, scope and evidential sufficiency across different product categories.
3. Distinguish failures of reasoning from failures of concept identification, language interpretation, evidence extraction and attribution. Do not treat these as the same problem.
4. Reuse and strengthen the existing declarative logic and knowledge structures. Consider description-logic and first-order-logic principles where they provide a concrete benefit, but do not introduce a new reasoner or ontology merely for theoretical completeness.
5. Demonstrate actual knowledge consultation through controlled knowledge-on/knowledge-off ablations. The same algorithms must behave differently because valid scientific knowledge is present, not because new vocabulary or category-specific code was added.
6. Where the structured reasoning works, improve the connection from consumer wording and document observations to the existing scientific concepts and relations. Preserve negation, quantities, conditions and uncertainty.
7. Continue the previously authorised evidence-validation extension in experimental/shadow mode, reusing the same general scientific principles rather than adding publisher-specific exceptions. Do not enable new public ranking paths.
8. Validate with counterexamples, cross-category cases and genuinely independent material when already available. Never use S80 D/H as fresh validation, and do not acquire new external pages without an authorised budget.
9. Preserve existing safeguards, the private knowledge store, deterministic decisions, provenance, and previous verified capabilities. Use existing ECC skills selectively when they add value; avoid redundant agent loops.
10. Report separately:

* what the existing system could already infer;
* what was corrected or implemented;
* what improvement comes specifically from scientific knowledge;
* what generalises across categories;
* what remains unverified.

Success criterion: demonstrate at least one reusable scientific principle that PEE applies correctly across different situations using acquired knowledge, with an observable improvement over the same system without that knowledge.

If this cannot be demonstrated, identify the precise architectural or representational blocker rather than adding more specialised rules or experiments.

Keep the R1–R7 roadmap intact. R3 does not close merely because tests pass or extraction improves.
