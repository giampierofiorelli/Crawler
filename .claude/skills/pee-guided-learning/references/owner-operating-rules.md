# PEE owner decisions — current operational precedence

**Status**: Owner-decision extraction from cumulative research v9 (15 October 2026). Reconcile with any later explicit owner decisions and repository facts before use.

1. **Single Claude developer now.** Research-guided category learning, semantic comprehension, code repair, gap-directed research and T1–T3 evaluation may proceed under Claude alone. No other AI roles are required and no external reviewer is authorized as an automated development dependency.
2. **Defer cross-agent proposals.** Research v6 proposed ChatGPT reviewer; research v7 proposed Claude↔Codex/PR integration. Research v8/v9 explicitly reversed those as **current operational instructions**. Preserve them as optional history; do not enable now.
3. **Do not discard v1–v5.** Their substantive research and teaching proposals remain active. Version 9 clarifies that separately blind human adjudication may not be claimed by a single Claude developer; lack of T4 is not a universal blocker to development or appropriate recommendations.
4. **Phase249 precedence.** At last research snapshot it was incomplete/unpublished. New work starts by verifying the *current* finalized Phase249 or later phase status; report discrepancies rather than replacing the current branch with obsolete snapshots.
5. **Knowledge is not capability.** A stored product specification differs from verified independent performance. A category hypothesis differs from evidence-supported relation. A Claude reasoning demonstration differs from a functioning deployed PEE runtime.
6. **Sensible protections, no paralysis.** Investigate overstrict false negatives and errors, but do not fabricate product identity, source attribution, measurement comparability or source rights. Do not publish data whose permissible use has not been established.
7. **Research is cumulative and critically revisable.** Evaluate new evidence against old; preserve originals and links, and document what is superseded and why. Use primary sources and transparent evaluation.
8. **English first, existing architecture first, minimal dependencies, one repository, private durable SQLite.** Do not install large models, proprietary scraping/search services, or new infrastructure without justified evidence and authorization.
9. **Daily execution autonomy within boundaries.** The owner need not approve routine code changes, research iterations or internal tests. Escalate material legal, security, spending or architectural decision points.
