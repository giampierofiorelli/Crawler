# Third-party text in this directory

The request texts in `journey-real-requests-set4.json`, in the result files whose names start with
`journey-real-requests-set4`, and in the files under `development/`, are excerpts of questions posted on Stack Exchange
(cooking and coffee .stackexchange.com). They were obtained through the official Stack
Exchange API, not by crawling the sites, whose robots.txt disallows crawlers. They are shared under the Creative Commons
BY-SA licence version given for each case (CC BY-SA 2.5, 3.0 or 4.0). Each case names its author and links the original
question. They are used as evaluation inputs only (not for training or search indexing). Files under `development/` also
repeat request texts from earlier phases, covered by the notices in `../phase249/`, `../phase250/` and `../phase251/`. All other content
in this directory is PEE's own work.
