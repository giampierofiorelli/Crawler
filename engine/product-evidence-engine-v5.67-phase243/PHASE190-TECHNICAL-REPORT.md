# Product Evidence Engine — Phase190: Robust preference & evidence-envelope audit

**Date:** 12 October 2026  
**Base:** Canonical PEE v5.13 / Phase189.  
**Status:** Research-only module; **no product ranking authorized, no original rights or scientific-source admission granted.**

## Executive verdict

Phase190 adds a mathematically bounded **pairwise robustness audit** that is distinct from Phase189's Pareto-on-observed-points pilot. It tests whether the sign of a comparison remains the same across an explicitly supplied feasible envelope of *user preferences* and *measurement scenarios*. Strong functionality is intentionally withheld if original measurements, context, variant, units, mandatory suitability, or test protocols cannot be matched. This is conditional scenario reasoning—not statistical confidence, a complete ranking or evidence that consumer outcomes improve.

This module is original TypeScript inspired by robust ordinal regression and the algorithmic audit of user-uploaded Scikit-Criteria; no third-party repository was copied or installed.

## Why the technical change matters

Phase189 can verify strict Pareto dominance across exactly comparable observed measures. It cannot answer whether a pair with conflicting advantages remains preferred under different legitimate user priorities, nor whether interval-valued inputs cross a mandatory decision threshold. Phase190 now computes the **exact minimum and maximum** of the pairwise weighted utility difference under independent nonnegative preference weight ranges whose values sum to one. It uses the greedy extreme-point optimizer for a box-constrained simplex. All values must use **fixed, externally reviewable property anchors**, rather than ranges calculated from whichever products happened to be included. Tests include an exhaustive-grid cross-check.

Important limitation: the mathematical extrema are exact for the *declared model*; the normalization endpoints, bounds and weight envelopes in the evaluation are **analyst-created and have not been scientifically calibrated or externally reviewed**. In particular, a reported value in the corpus is not a measurement confidence interval.

## Engineering audit features

- Goal must be explicitly marked confirmed in this research scenario. Actual Phase177 server-signature linkage is **not** part of this module.
- Strict category, product/variant, field, protocol/version, test condition and physical unit scope.
- One original measurement per field and variant; conflicting duplicates or dependent originals are held, not cherry-picked or treated as independent corroboration.
- A material unknown is not a failed performance and cannot be imputed as zero.
- Noncompensatory technical vetoes: failure of a mandatory threshold on *any* considered field overrides utility tradeoffs; a bound crossing the threshold stays unresolved.
- Explicit restrictions on weights and source-bound numeric ranges, including infeasibility and out-of-scale safeguards.
- Pairwise comparisons do not depend on a third candidate; no hidden candidate-set min/max normalization or inherited overall-score rank.
- Outputs include `publicRankingAllowed:false` and readable limitations. Even `robustly_a_on_selected_criteria` does not mean A is the best product in the category.

## Reproducible pilot and results

File: `evaluation/phase190/research-scenario-results.json`, generated by `node scripts/phase190_demo.mjs` after `npm run build`. Uses the **same four manual RTINGS observations** (Dyson V15 Detect vs Shark Stratos IZ862H maximum hose airflow and bare-floor noise, methodology v1.4) already described in Phase189. The manually annotated source pages are:

- https://www.rtings.com/vacuum/reviews/dyson/v15-detect
- https://www.rtings.com/vacuum/reviews/shark/stratos-cordless
- https://www.rtings.com/vacuum/learn/how-we-test

**Observed scope:** the illustrative fixed endpoints and weight envelope produce `robustly_a_on_selected_criteria` for those two fields only. Adding a missing carpet-performance criterion produces `missing_or_conflicting_evidence`, not an imputed score; a second incompatible reading for one field also holds; unconfirmed shopper requirements do not authorize evaluation. These are research checks on manually paraphrased source facts and synthetic alterations, not independent lab replication, publisher text acquisition or human-supervised public recommendations.

**35 focused TypeScript tests**: deliberate tradeoff preference reversals, stability across feasible weights, failures of mandatory thresholds, unknown threshold bands, missing values, conflicting originals, reused measurement roots, exact SKU/condition/protocol/units, utility anchors, infeasible weights, permutations, candidate insertion invariance, real-source scope checks across vacuums, laptops and headphones, and deterministic optimization compared against a dense grid. All focused tests passed before cumulative regressions. **No blinded cross-system performance conclusion** should be inferred from these engineering tests.

## What is still missing

1. Independent original-document custody, publisher permissions, exact retail-variant and conflict verification; own page and publisher's own summary are **not independent lab confirmations**.
2. Measured test uncertainty, sampling variation, validated preference elicitation, source-specific cross-method comparability, independently approved utility scales; missing data and near ties demand even more conservative handling.
3. Real held-out benchmark with independent domain reviewers, authentic shopper questions, source coverage audit, tested interface and outcomes such as wrong-veto rate / regret / unsupported winner rate.
4. Live integration with signed review policy and Phase181 ranking: not activated. This module must not bypass Phases169–171, 177, 180, 185–189.
5. ASReview-style document screening needs a large screened source corpus with expert recall labels, notably decisive negative documents; pgmpy needs calibrated priors and held-out predictive validation. More lines of code are not the target by themselves.

## Proposed Phase191

Freeze a reviewed original professional-source corpus across at least two independent labs or a legally reusable benchmark. Obtain signed original source custody and lab protocol/variant crosswalk. Develop **independent benchmark design with a blind gold set**, estimate defensible metric/weight uncertainty, and test calibrated preference robustness against existing Phase181/187/188/189 outputs without changing public ranking unless there is measured benefit. Continue category generalization, including practical professional observations and manufacturer specs as appropriate, and evaluate whether source screening helps actual coverage.
