# Phase191 — Scarce evidence without source laundering

This is a **research-only, read-only overlay**, implemented as `src/phase191_sparse_source_integrity.ts` on Phase189's exact-product, condition-specific source dossier. It does not replace source rights custody (Phases169–171), externally signed Phase187 field certification or Phase181 dynamic ranking.

## Critical distinction

**Unstudied ≠ poor.** Preserve known candidates with no applicable observation, and preserve manufacturer specifications as attributed provisional facts. Avoid an arbitrary minimum number of reviews: one original test may support a narrowly defined measurement, but cannot establish complete coverage or overall excellence.

**Commercial association ≠ factual falsity.** Record publisher ownership, finance/sponsorship, method, product supply and provenance separately. Crucially, every *individual claim* must have a distinct origin: an independent editorial publisher may simply relay a manufacturer's numbers. No site-level badge can certify the claim's source. No claim provenance is inferred from a product's brand or domain string. Suspected commercial bias requires inspection, not invented penalty percentages. Commercial promotional sources can be helpful discovery leads but cannot become independent original lab results.

**Research annotation ≠ admitted evidence.** All Phase189 external-page observations are manual paraphrases. No publisher originals or reuse rights, no signed independent review, no public product ranking. Truthfulness, real test independence and provenance cannot be proven by a local string label. None of the code authorizes exposing a ranking or hard exclusion.

## Outputs

For each requested product and precise technical field: `knowledgeState`, `visibility` (always retained), exact scope, unique root count (diagnostic, not vote), commercial signals, limited threshold outcome only for eligible reported original methods, missingness and targeted research actions. The `origins` input is independently supplied per claim, to prevent source laundering. Existing Phase189 assessor remains the source of threshold matching, never rewritten.

## Threat model / tested categories

Synthetic manufacturer-only and sponsored dehumidifier cases, plus original Phase189 annotated vacuum, laptop and headphone publications. Tests check missing SKU/category, original-test re-publication, sponsored editorial, affiliate and loan disclosures, conflicting test roots, method mismatch, field condition mismatch and deterministic permutation. All synthetic cases are labeled fixtures and must not appear as real product facts.

## Relevant independent methodology

- Cochrane [Handbook ch. 7](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-07): risk of bias, conflicts, missing results and imprecision are distinct; avoid indiscriminate summed quality scores. **This is not a GRADE assessment of consumer products.**
- US FTC [Endorsement Guides](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking): material relationship disclosure, including affiliates.
- UK ASA/CAP [Recognition of marketing communications](https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/02.html) and [Affiliate marketing](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/affiliate-marketing.html): advertised content should be identifiable. **These do not independently rate laboratory quality.**
- W3C [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/): provenance modeling concepts; Phase188/189 dependency grouping reused.

## No-go limitations

No blinded held-out external labels, empirical calibration of source quality, independent original-source audit, licensing approval or full marketplace coverage. Phase191 records are *not* ready to drive hard vetoes or final rank. Next: independent source verification, source-level/claim-level disclosures on more varied categories, benchmark with actual independent reviewer adjudication, and later signed policy admission when evidence is adequate.
