# Phase198 source basis (checked 2026-10-13)

The following links are **primary documentation of published schemes, methodological standards or recommendations**. They do not independently verify any particular person's qualification, laboratory measurements, product certification, impartiality or product performance. Publisher statements are evidence about the *stated scheme*, not proof that each review passes it.

| Source | Original URL | What it actually supports; limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana | https://www.pizzanapoletana.org/en/forni_elettrici_approvati | Existence of an approved-supplier/oven register explicitly scoped to Neapolitan pizza. Not a universal oven ranking or independently audited source of comparative scores. |
| Specialty Coffee Association, Certified Home Equipment | https://sca.coffee/certified-home-brewer | Third-party testing/registered models for **home coffee brewers**, not an endorsement of Italian espresso preparation. Exact model identification is necessary. |
| MAFF Japan, certification of cooking skills | https://www.maff.go.jp/e/policies/market/cooking_skills.html | Framework for assessment of persons competent in Japanese cuisine overseas; MAFF's guidance does **not** certify any knife or its performance. |
| ISO 8586:2023 (official abstract) | https://www.iso.org/standard/76667.html | Selection and training of trained/expert sensory assessors; not a passport or country-of-origin credibility rule. Full normative text may require a licence. |
| ISO 11132:2021 (official abstract) | https://www.iso.org/standard/76669.html | Monitoring discrimination/agreement/repeatability for specified quantitative descriptive panels; not all tasting formats. |
| ISO on certification bodies | https://committee.iso.org/bodies.html | ISO/IEC 17065 conformity-assessment requirements, impartiality and scope of product/process/service certification bodies. Presence of a marketing badge does not establish accreditation. |
| ISO, attestations of conformity | https://committee.iso.org/attestations-of-conformity.html | Test reports, conformity claims, certificates and marks carry distinct meanings; validity and controlled mark use matter. |
| W3C WAI, disabled users and evaluation | https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/involving-users/ | First-hand user evaluation can find usability issues missed by conformance checks. Such testing does not replace standards and is not universally generalisable. |
| W3C PROV-O | https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/ | Distinguishes attribution, primary source, derivation, activity and agent; does not authenticate externally supplied test-event IDs. |

## Evidence vs engineering inference

The published schemes and standards justify *modelling* skill, outcome scope, test method, original event and registry decision independently. **No source claims Phase198's rule set has been scientifically calibrated**, and no new source here grants blanket authority to a nationality, person, association or publisher. The optional-vs-essential contract, record checks and research-only overlay are PEE engineering design decisions pending real blind review.
