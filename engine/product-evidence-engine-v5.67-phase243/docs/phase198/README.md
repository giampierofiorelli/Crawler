# Phase198 — Specialist Outcome Validation

New source: `src/phase198_specialist_outcome_validation.ts`. Main API: `assessSpecialistOutcome198`, `specialistJobHints198`. Targeted tests: `tests/phase198_specialist_outcome_validation.test.ts`.

Purpose: a **scoped, reviewer-annotated extra layer** for product outcomes where professional practical use, established sensory competence or the exact scope of a specialist product programme materially matters to the buyer. No global author/publisher authority score; no automatic certification; no live registry lookup, translation, evidence admission, ranking change or raw review retention.

The Phase197 call runs first, so safety and compatibility, prior gaps, previous search boundary and original-event annotations retain their meaning. Requirements must name a reviewed user goal, exact criterion, discipline and outcome. Optional corroboration cannot hold all product research hostage. Essential criteria cannot be compensated by excellent unrelated technical specifications. Suspended/expired/unreviewed programmes, sponsorships, personal credentials or general endorsements cannot impersonate a scoped product-outcome test.

Structured professional observations require the Phase197/191 same-record review lineage, documented specialist practice for the exact discipline/outcome, a reviewed method, actual operator participation and conflict review. A controlled sensory panel additionally requires evidence of panel capability, not just practitioner reputation. Scheme recognition requires an existing Phase197 official-programme record, exact product variant, reviewed registry, scope, method, programme version and validity. All such records stay **reviewed-unadmitted annotations**; no original experiment provenance has been externally authenticated.

For a given criterion, result/root counts are deduplicated and a same-event lab measurement is not presented as an independent corroborating event. Any qualified contrary outcome produces a research-review gap. `specialistJobHints198` maps uncovered criteria only to existing Phase182 exact-variant jobs and Phase196 matrix candidates, and cannot generate an unauthorized queue item.

Known limitations: operator-supplied annotation IDs are not proven authentic; programme website content/credentials not retrieved or cross-verified; no learned expertise model; no domain-exhaustive competence ontology; no translation pipeline; no independent bench validation or causal improvement in purchases shown. The real corpus assessment protocol belongs to Phase199.
