**No new expert evidence was approved.** EPA ENERGY STAR program data must retain `certification_registry_manufacturer_reported` evidence class; certified-program participation is not equivalent to an independent side-by-side consumer-lab test. The room area field is *derived* from smoke CADR, not independent corroboration. No value has been converted into normalized scoring points. Unique EPA identifiers do not by themselves prove matching retail variants. The source cannot prove gas/VOC removal, noise at matched conditions, filter lifetime, prices or individual health improvements. The present output is quarantine/staging, not a Phase181 product catalog and **not a ranking**.
