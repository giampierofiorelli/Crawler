## HARD PRODUCT AND EVIDENCE RULES
- **Independent product assessment/ranking FIRST.** Prices, retailers, Amazon, commercial commissions and affiliate availability NEVER influence the ranking. Merchants and affiliate links are downstream, optional 'where to buy' information.
- For first launch, **no live prices, list-price/budget filters presented as verified prices, historical-price estimates, buy-now/wait predictions or deal-monitoring**. Historical experimental price modules exist in repository but MUST remain quarantined. Do not reactivate them inadvertently. Budget/price is relevant to shopping but deferred until legally viable data and method exist.
- Technical specifications count ONLY when clearly mapped to genuine, version/variant-specific manufacturer or regulatory **technical documentation**; label as *manufacturer-declared*, never claim they are measured. Manufacturer marketing/sales/performance rhetoric is NOT valid evidence of performance.
- Independent evidence: original third-party laboratory tests and separately labeled professional editorial user-experience assessments, preserving original method, product variant, protocol/version, context, rights and uncertainty. No fabricated measurements, ratings, permissions, coefficients or repeatability.
- **No double counting:** ten syndicated articles reporting one original laboratory test are one original test, not ten independent confirmations. Distinguish different protocols, conditions, variants and original raw datasets. Missing test != bad product. An inconclusive or context-mismatched test != a valid universal conclusion.
- Professional original test evidence is the primary source for product performance. Consumer/user reviews are an **exceptional, selectively triggered** source of individual lived-experience observations only when the exact user need remains unanswered and such observations might be decision-material. They do not substitute for controlled measurement or represent statistically reliable prevalence. An LLM's extraction must be grounded in the original passage and checked for negation, context, traceability, bias and rights. No user review automatically enters ranking.
- In the current experimental build Amazon product content, ratings and user reviews are entirely excluded from research/ranking. Affiliate links ONLY downstream, under separately checked program terms. The user's current exploratory web-browsing proposal is NOT consent to violate these restrictions.
- New criteria, weights, reliability probabilities or 'expert agreement' cannot be invented. Test observations must be authentic; constructed software fixtures are NOT empirical validation. Maintain a held-out human-reviewed evaluation and report unknowns honestly.
- No unjustified categorical statements that all professional review access/reasoning/citation is unlawful. Equally, no claim that paraphrasing, citing or not storing guarantees lawful access/reuse. Research on each relevant mode of use.
- All findings, important data points, legal or technical claims and quotations must have verifiable **copyable URLs** and clear source context. If making a file/report, put URL links *inside the file*, not only external citation markers.
